Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

The short version: your songs stay on your iPhone. Saucebox has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send your recordings, lyrics or any other data to us or anyone else.

What Saucebox stores

Everything you make in Saucebox (songs, takes, recordings, lyrics, tags and notes) is saved in the app's own storage on your iPhone. We have no server and never receive a copy.

Like other app data, it may be included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backups if you have them turned on. Those backups are run by Apple under your own settings and Apple's privacy policy.

Microphone

Saucebox asks for microphone access so it can record your takes and run the tuner. The microphone is used only while you are recording or tuning, and the audio is saved only on your iPhone.

Speech recognition

Saucebox can turn your recorded takes into lyric drafts. It uses Apple's speech recognition in on-device mode, so your audio is processed on your iPhone and is not uploaded for transcription.

Sharing

When you tap Share, Saucebox prepares your takes and a short summary of the song, then opens the standard iOS share sheet. Your files go only where you choose (for example Mail, Messages, AirDrop or Files), and the app or service you pick handles them under its own privacy policy. Nothing is shared unless you start it.

What we collect

Nothing. Saucebox has no sign-in and no third-party code that collects data, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

TestFlight beta

If you are testing a beta through Apple's TestFlight app, Apple may share some information with us to help improve the app: crash reports, basic device and usage details, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. This is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy and the TestFlight terms. We use it only to fix problems and improve Saucebox.

Children

Saucebox does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If Saucebox ever starts collecting data, we will say so here before it happens.
